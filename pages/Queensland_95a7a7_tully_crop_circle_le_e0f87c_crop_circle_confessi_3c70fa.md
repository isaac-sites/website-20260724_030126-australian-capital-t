## Introduction

The 1991 confession by British crop-circle makers Doug Bower and Dave Chorley fundamentally changed how claims about crop circles were evaluated. Their admission demonstrated that convincing circular formations could be created secretly, overnight, using simple hand tools rather than exotic technology or unexplained forces. That shifted the burden of proof: the existence of a striking circular pattern was no longer, by itself, persuasive evidence of an extraordinary event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1991/09/11/artists-claim-they-made-mystery-crop-circles/a0a5503e-2a38-4223-80e1-5684c43561ac/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: washingtonpost.com">[The Washington Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">washingtonpost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Washington Post ARTISTS CLAIM THEY MADE MYSTERY CROP CIRCLES</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Washington PostARTISTS CLAIM THEY MADE MYSTERY CROP CIRCLES - The Washington PostSeptember 11, 1991...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 11, 1991</span></span></span>

For [Queensland]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-queenslands-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s UFO history, however, the confession had a more limited significance than is often assumed. Bower and Chorley acknowledged that the 1966 Tully "[saucer nest]({{ 'saucer-nest/' | relative_url }})" story had inspired their own English creations, linking Horseshoe Lagoon to the later crop-circle phenomenon. Yet their admission did not explain what actually happened at Tully. It weakened broad claims about crop circles as evidence of alien activity while leaving the original Queensland reed disturbance unresolved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop_circle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crop circle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Crop circle</span></span></span>

## How the confession changed the burden of proof

Before 1991, many discussions of British crop circles centred on whether unusual patterns required unusual explanations. The confession altered that debate by providing a documented human method for producing impressive formations that many observers had regarded as mysterious.

Bower and Chorley described creating circles with little more than wooden planks, rope and a simple sighting device attached to a cap to maintain straight lines and accurate geometry. They also demonstrated their techniques publicly, showing that carefully planned designs could be completed without sophisticated equipment. In one widely discussed episode, a leading believer initially judged a freshly made demonstration circle to be genuine before learning that it had been created by the confessed hoaxers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop_circle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crop circle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Crop circle</span></span></span>

This did not prove that every crop circle had been made by the pair, nor that every reported formation was fraudulent. Instead, it established a crucial evidential principle: spectacular appearance alone could no longer be treated as evidence of an extraordinary cause when an ordinary method had been shown to produce similar results.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop_circle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crop circle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Crop circle</span></span></span>

## What simple tools demonstrated about British circles

The importance of the confession lay less in the specific number of circles Bower and Chorley claimed to have made than in what their methods demonstrated.

Their work showed that:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--caution" markdown="1">

* large circular patterns could be produced with inexpensive, readily available tools;
* formations could be made overnight without attracting widespread attention;
* careful planning could create designs that appeared remarkably precise;
* experienced investigators could sometimes mistake human-made formations for unexplained ones when relying only on visual inspection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop_circle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crop circle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Crop circle</span></span></span>

</div>

The confession also encouraged a broader sceptical reassessment of crop-circle reports. Researchers increasingly recognised that each new formation required independent evidence rather than assuming that unusual geometry implied an unknown physical process or extraterrestrial involvement. Human artistry, imitation and deliberate hoaxing had become demonstrated possibilities rather than theoretical objections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop_circle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crop circle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Crop circle</span></span></span>

## Why the admission did not explain Horseshoe Lagoon

Despite frequent references to Tully in discussions of crop circles, the 1991 confession did not solve Queensland's original case.

The key difference is that the 1966 Tully incident involved a disturbed area of floating reeds in a lagoon rather than flattened cereal crops in an English field. Bower and Chorley never claimed to have created the Queensland formation. Instead, they said reports of the Tully "saucer nest" inspired them to begin making crop circles in England years later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop_circle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crop circle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Crop circle</span></span></span>

That distinction matters because inspiration is not explanation. Their confession demonstrated that later British circles could be manufactured, but it provided no evidence about the physical mechanism responsible for the Horseshoe Lagoon disturbance.

Several possibilities for the Queensland case had already been discussed independently of later crop-circle hoaxes, including whirlwinds, waterspouts, downdraughts and other natural processes affecting wetland vegetation. None of those explanations depended on the existence or non-existence of British hoaxers. Likewise, the confession offered no new observations, measurements or eyewitness testimony about the original 1966 event. The evidence for Tully therefore remained exactly as it had been before: a documented reed disturbance, an eyewitness account of an unidentified object, and competing interpretations that could not be conclusively resolved from the surviving evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop_circle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crop circle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Crop circle</span></span></span>

## What the confession means for Tully's place in Queensland's UFO history

Within Queensland's UFO history, the lasting significance of the 1991 confession is interpretive rather than evidential.

It reinforces the need to separate two different historical questions:

* **What happened at Horseshoe Lagoon in January 1966?** That remains an unresolved historical case with competing explanations.
* **What did later crop-circle culture demonstrate?** It showed that visually impressive circular formations can be produced deliberately by people using straightforward methods.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop_circle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crop circle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Crop circle</span></span></span>

Keeping those questions separate avoids two common errors. One is to argue that because many later crop circles were human-made, Tully must also have been a hoax. The other is to argue that because Tully remains uncertain, later crop circles should therefore be treated as unexplained. Neither conclusion follows from the available evidence.

The confession ultimately weakened sweeping claims that crop circles, as a class of phenomena, pointed towards extraterrestrial activity. It did not, however, establish the cause of Queensland's original "saucer nest". For that reason, Tully continues to occupy an unusual position in Australian UFO history: not as proof of alien visitation, but as an unresolved local incident whose cultural influence reached far beyond the evidence available for the event itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop_circle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crop circle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Crop circle</span></span></span>

    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
