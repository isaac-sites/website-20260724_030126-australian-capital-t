## Introduction

The [Northern Territory]({{ 'why-is-the-northern-territory-a-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s exceptionally dark skies make satellites far easier to notice than in most Australian cities. That is one reason why sincere UFO reports sometimes begin with a real object in orbit rather than an aircraft or atmospheric phenomenon. Modern satellite constellations, especially SpaceX's Starlink network, have introduced new patterns that many people have never seen before: lines of evenly spaced lights, objects that brighten and fade without warning, and lights that appear to vanish in an instant. None of these observations automatically explain every unidentified sighting, but they have become one of the most common conventional explanations that investigators now check first.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://heavens-above.com/faq.aspx?alt=205.0&amp;cul=en&amp;lat=49.3147222222&amp;lng=-121.2625&amp;loc=Hope+Slide&amp;tz=PST" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: heavens-above.com">[Heavens-Above]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">heavens-above.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Frequently asked questions (FAQ</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Frequently asked questions (FAQ)...</span></span></span>

## Why satellites are brightest near twilight

Most satellites are only visible because they reflect sunlight. The observer on the ground must already be in darkness while the spacecraft remains high enough to stay illuminated by the Sun. Those conditions usually occur for a few hours after sunset and before sunrise. Around midnight, many low-Earth-orbit satellites have entered Earth's shadow and become invisible, even though they are still overhead.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://heavens-above.com/faq.aspx?alt=205.0&amp;cul=en&amp;lat=49.3147222222&amp;lng=-121.2625&amp;loc=Hope+Slide&amp;tz=PST" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: heavens-above.com">[Heavens-Above]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">heavens-above.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Frequently asked questions (FAQ</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Frequently asked questions (FAQ)...</span></span></span>

## How Starlink formations change after launch

Starlink has introduced a new kind of sighting that was almost unknown before 2019. Shortly after launch, dozens of satellites occupy nearly the same orbit before gradually spreading out over days and weeks.

To an observer on the ground, a newly launched group can appear as:

* evenly spaced objects moving at identical speed;
* a "train" stretching across a large section of sky;

## Why Starlink can look stranger than ordinary satellites

Several characteristics distinguish Starlink from traditional satellite observations.

First, the sheer number of spacecraft means multiple satellites may be visible during a single observing session. In very dark Northern Territory skies, it is no longer unusual to see several unrelated satellites crossing different parts of the sky within minutes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.04328" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visibility Predictions for Near-Future Satellite Megaconstellations: Latitudes near 50 Degrees will Experience the Worst Light Pollu...</span></span></span>

Second, Starlink satellites sometimes change brightness noticeably as their solar panels and body orientation alter the amount of sunlight reflected toward the observer. A light may brighten unexpectedly, fade, or become difficult to see without changing course. Although dramatic, these changes arise from viewing geometry rather than unusual flight behaviour.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.03226" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on arxiv.org.</span></span></span>

Third, people often expect aircraft to display flashing navigation lights or make engine noise. Satellites show neither. Instead they move silently and steadily, reinforcing the impression that they are something fundamentally different.

## How orbital data can reconstruct a report

Modern UFO investigations have an advantage unavailable to many earlier investigators: precise orbital predictions.

When a report includes an accurate date, time and observing location, investigators can compare it with publicly available satellite databases. A convincing identification normally requires agreement on several points simultaneously:

## What satellite matches can and cannot prove

Matching a UFO report with satellite data can provide a highly persuasive explanation, but it does not prove that every unexplained report involves satellites.

Some reports fail to match any predicted pass because:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the recorded time is inaccurate;
* the witness estimated direction incorrectly;
* the observation involved an aircraft, meteor or atmospheric effect instead;
* the reported behaviour genuinely differs from known satellite motion.

</div>

Likewise, the existence of Starlink should not become a catch-all explanation for every unusual light. Good investigations test satellite hypotheses carefully and reject them when the timing, track or appearance does not fit. The strongest conclusions come from comparing witness testimony with independent observational data rather than assuming either extraordinary or ordinary explanations from the outset.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufouap.net/en/explainers/starlink-satellites-ufo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufouap.net">[ufouap.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufouap.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufouap.net.</span></span></span>

  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
