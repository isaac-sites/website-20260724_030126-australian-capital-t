## Introduction

The reported Dalwallinu sighting of January 1953 occupies an important place in [Western Australia]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-western/' | relative_url }})'s UFO history not because officials confirmed an extraordinary object, but because newspapers showed readers how a mysterious claim could be tested. Rather than simply repeating witness testimony, the reporting described consultations with the Government Astronomer, the Royal Australian Air Force (RAAF) at Pearce and air traffic control at Guildford. Those enquiries failed to identify the reported object, making the incident an early example of the style of reporting that later shaped Western Australian UFO waves: eyewitness accounts accompanied by official checks, yet stopping short of claiming proof of anything unusual.

Within the broader history of how newspapers influenced Western Australia's UFO stories, the Dalwallinu case became significant because it demonstrated both the value and the limits of official investigation. The inability to provide an immediate explanation made the story more newsworthy, but it did not establish that an unknown craft had been present.

## What four Dalwallinu witnesses reportedly saw

According to a *Daily News* report published in January 1953, four residents near Dalwallinu reported seeing what the newspaper described as a "flying saucer" in the western and south-western sky. The available surviving summaries do not preserve a detailed reconstruction of the object's appearance or [flight path]({{ 'flight-path/' | relative_url }}), but the fact that multiple witnesses reportedly observed the same phenomenon gave the story greater credibility than a single-person account.

For newspaper readers in 1953, this mattered. The "flying saucer" phenomenon had already attracted international attention after the late-1940s reports from the United States, and Australian newspapers increasingly treated local observations as potentially significant public-interest stories. At the same time, editors recognised that extraordinary claims needed supporting context if they were to be taken seriously.

Rather than relying solely on the witnesses' descriptions, the article immediately turned to expert opinion and official records.

## What astronomers and aviation officials could rule out

The strength of the newspaper coverage came from the enquiries it described rather than from any dramatic new evidence.

The *Daily News* reportedly sought comment from Government Astronomer H. S. Spigl. Based on the description provided to him, he reportedly considered that the object appeared to be travelling too fast to fit an ordinary astronomical explanation such as a meteorite. This was a cautious assessment rather than a declaration that the object was genuinely anomalous. * **RAAF Pearce**, which stated that it had no aircraft operating in the area at the relevant time.
* **Guildford air traffic control**, which likewise reported no known aircraft matching the sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://thewest.com.au/news/wa/was-ufo-history-revealed-state-records-show-the-skies-over-wa-have-seen-many-unidentified-flying-objects-c-8450517" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thewest.com.au">[The West Australian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thewest.com.au</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The West AustralianUntold history of WA: State records show the skies over WA have seen many UFOs &#124; The West AustralianNovember 5, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 5, 2022</span></span></span>

These checks ruled out one obvious class of explanation: a routine aircraft known to local aviation authorities. Importantly, however, they did not eliminate every conventional possibility. Civilian aircraft outside the available records, observational errors, unusual atmospheric conditions or inaccurate estimates of speed and distance could still not be excluded.

This distinction often disappears in later retellings, but it is central to understanding the case historically.

## Why an unexplained result was not proof of a craft

The Dalwallinu report illustrates an important principle in evaluating historical UFO cases: an unexplained observation is not the same as evidence for an extraordinary vehicle.

The official enquiries described in the newspaper were necessarily limited. They relied on available records and the witnesses' recollections rather than physical evidence, radar tracking, photographs or recovered material. The astronomer's opinion addressed whether the reported motion resembled a familiar astronomical object, while the aviation enquiries addressed whether known aircraft were present. Neither investigation could positively identify what had been seen.

In other words, the enquiries narrowed some possibilities without producing a definitive answer.

This distinction remains important when reading both contemporary newspapers and later UFO literature. Some later accounts emphasise that astronomers and aviation authorities could not explain the sighting, but that is different from saying those authorities endorsed an extraterrestrial or otherwise extraordinary interpretation. The historical record supports only the more limited conclusion that no immediate explanation emerged from the enquiries reported at the time.

## Why the reporting mattered more than the outcome

The Dalwallinu case became influential less because of the sighting itself than because of how it was presented.

The reporting established a pattern that would appear repeatedly in Western Australian UFO coverage over the following decades:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* several apparently independent witnesses;
* consultation with recognised experts;
* checks with aviation authorities;
* acknowledgement that obvious explanations had been considered;
* an unresolved ending rather than a sensational conclusion.

</div>

That structure encouraged readers to see the story as investigated rather than merely speculative. It also gave journalists a way to report unusual claims without endorsing them. The absence of an explanation became part of the news, while the official enquiries demonstrated that someone had attempted to verify the account.

This reporting style would later become familiar during periods of increased UFO reporting in Western Australia, where newspapers frequently combined eyewitness testimony with police, aviation or astronomical comment before leaving readers with an unresolved question rather than a definitive answer.

    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
