## Introduction

In 1982, [New South Wales]({{ 'which-new-south-wales-ufo-cases-still/' | relative_url }}) investigator Bill Chalker became one of the first civilian researchers to spend extended time examining Australia's Commonwealth UFO records inside the Department of Defence in Canberra. His visit has since become an important episode in Australian UFO history, not because it produced a single dramatic revelation, but because it showed that official files contained far more material than many researchers had expected while also demonstrating how differently the same records could be interpreted.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/New_South_Wales_f92f26_civilian_ufo_archive_a2ad13_chalker_defence_file_c43729-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Chalker Files illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/New_South_Wales_f92f26_civilian_ufo_archive_a2ad13_chalker_defence_file_c43729-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/New_South_Wales_f92f26_civilian_ufo_archive_a2ad13_chalker_defence_file_c43729-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

For historians of New South Wales's civilian UFO movement, Chalker's work mattered because it bridged two worlds. It connected private investigators, who had spent years collecting witness reports, with government archives that had previously been difficult for outsiders to inspect. At the same time, the files highlighted an enduring debate: did they point towards genuinely unexplained aerial phenomena, or did they mainly document the routine administrative handling of unusual reports?<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-12-07/accessing-australia-secret-ufo-files/104673082" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.net.au">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.net.au</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC NewsHow Bill Chalker became one of the first civilians to access the RAAF&#x27;s UFO files - ABC NewsDecember 7, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2024</span></span></span>

## How Chalker gained access to the Canberra records

By the early 1980s, Bill Chalker had already established himself as a serious civilian investigator based in New South Wales. Recovering from surgery in 1981, he began a determined campaign of letters and telephone calls seeking access to Defence UFO files that had long been inaccessible to most members of the public.

After roughly a year of persistence, the Department of Defence granted him permission to examine records at the Russell Offices in Canberra. Chalker deliberately approached the visit as a formal research exercise rather than an exercise in sensationalism. Contemporary accounts describe him arriving dressed for a professional meeting and spending approximately a week reviewing files, taking extensive notes and copying material that had either been declassified or could be released.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-12-07/accessing-australia-secret-ufo-files/104673082" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.net.au">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.net.au</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC NewsHow Bill Chalker became one of the first civilians to access the RAAF&#x27;s UFO files - ABC NewsDecember 7, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2024</span></span></span>

His visit was unusual because the records were still held by Defence rather than the National Archives. This gave him access to files before many were more widely available through later archival releases.

## What the copied files added to civilian research

The most important contribution was not proof of extraterrestrial visitors but access to primary documents that civilian researchers had never previously seen.

Among the material Chalker identified were:

* official correspondence showing how the Royal Australian Air Force (RAAF) received, assessed and circulated UFO reports;
* investigation files on military and civilian sightings;
* references to scientific assessments commissioned by Defence;
* [evidence]({{ 'evidence/' | relative_url }}) that the surviving files represented only part of the government's overall collection.

One of the most discussed discoveries was repeated reference to a 1954 scientific assessment prepared by nuclear physicist Harry Turner for Air Force Intelligence. Turner reviewed early Australian cases and attempted to evaluate them using scientific rather than purely operational criteria. The report became historically significant because it showed that the RAAF had sought expert scientific advice during the early years of its UFO investigations rather than treating every report as mere public correspondence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-12-07/accessing-australia-secret-ufo-files/104673082" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.net.au">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.net.au</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC NewsHow Bill Chalker became one of the first civilians to access the RAAF&#x27;s UFO files - ABC NewsDecember 7, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2024</span></span></span>

Chalker also concluded that the files available to him represented only about one-third of the Defence Department's UFO holdings at the time. That finding encouraged him to submit more targeted requests over the following years, allowing additional records to emerge through formal access procedures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.net.au/listen/programs/expanse/the-ufo-files/104645758" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.net.au">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.net.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ABC News03 Uncropped &#124; The UFO files</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC listen...</span></span></span>

For civilian researchers in New South Wales, this mattered because it shifted discussion away from second-hand rumours towards documentary evidence. Instead of relying solely on witness recollections or newspaper reports, investigators could compare published stories against official memoranda, intelligence summaries and internal correspondence.

## What the files did not prove

Although later UFO literature sometimes presents Chalker's discoveries as exposing hidden government knowledge, the documentary record supports a more cautious conclusion.

The files clearly demonstrated that Australian defence authorities took some reports seriously enough to investigate, particularly when trained observers, military personnel, radar operators or aviation safety might be involved. They also showed that Defence sought technical advice and maintained correspondence with overseas organisations during parts of the Cold War.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-12-07/accessing-australia-secret-ufo-files/104673082" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.net.au">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.net.au</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC NewsHow Bill Chalker became one of the first civilians to access the RAAF&#x27;s UFO files - ABC NewsDecember 7, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2024</span></span></span>

However, the documents did not establish that Defence had confirmed extraterrestrial spacecraft.

In military reporting, "unidentified" generally meant that the available information was insufficient for identification, not that an alien origin had been established.

## Why researchers and former officials read them differently

The same archive produced sharply different interpretations.

Many civilian investigators viewed the files as evidence that governments had publicly downplayed a subject they privately regarded as worthy of careful attention. The existence of detailed investigations, scientific reviews and internal memoranda suggested that official interest had extended beyond what many people realised.

Former Defence personnel and officials often drew a different lesson. From their perspective, maintaining files on unusual aerial reports was an ordinary responsibility of defence and aviation organisations during the Cold War. Investigating reports that might involve aircraft, missiles, balloons or potential security concerns did not necessarily imply belief in extraordinary explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-12-07/accessing-australia-secret-ufo-files/104673082" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.net.au">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.net.au</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC NewsHow Bill Chalker became one of the first civilians to access the RAAF&#x27;s UFO files - ABC NewsDecember 7, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2024</span></span></span>

This disagreement remains central to understanding the Canberra records. Both sides generally agree on the authenticity of the documents themselves. The dispute concerns what those documents mean.

For believers, the files demonstrate that some cases resisted conventional explanation despite official investigation.

For sceptics, they mainly illustrate a bureaucracy recording uncertain observations, with the absence of a firm explanation reflecting limited evidence rather than hidden knowledge.

## A lasting example: the North West Cape files

One consequence of Chalker's archival work was the renewed examination of specific military cases that had received little public attention.

## Why Chalker's work remains significant for the New South Wales UFO record

Within the history of New South Wales civilian investigation, Bill Chalker's access to the Defence files marked an important transition from collecting stories to analysing government records.

His work strengthened civilian research by introducing original documentary sources into public discussion, enabling later investigators to compare witness accounts with official paperwork instead of relying entirely on secondary reports. Equally important, it highlighted the need for careful interpretation. Authentic government files undoubtedly existed and contained valuable historical evidence, but they did not provide simple answers about the nature of the reported phenomena.

  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
